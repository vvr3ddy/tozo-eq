Before you connect

The buds only advertise their BLE control channel in short windows. Tick these off first so the device shows up:

  • 1 Take the buds out of the case, or leave the lid open. A closed, charging case hides them.
  • 2 Force-close the TOZO app on your phone — it holds the control channel and blocks this page.
  • 3 Use a Chromium browser (Chrome, Edge, Vivaldi, Opera). Brave, Safari and Firefox don't support Web Bluetooth.
  • 4 Keep playing audio — it streams over normal Bluetooth (A2DP) the whole time. This is only a side channel.
